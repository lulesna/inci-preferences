Description of the Service

INCI Preferences is a web application that provides personalised analysis of cosmetic product ingredients based on user-defined preferences. The main features include:

  • personalised ingredient classification (safe, moderate, unsafe),
  • a cosmetic product database with search and filtering,
  • personalised product recommendations,
  • a similar product finder (dupes),
  • OCR-based ingredient scanning from photos,
  • favourite products management.
