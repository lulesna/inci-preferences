Ingredient safety judged by your skin, not by an average

Most ingredient databases give every substance one rating that everyone sees. Skin doesn't work like that. Niacinamide sits on almost every list as safe, and plenty of people with atopic skin still react to it.

So here you decide. Mark an ingredient once and every product in the catalogue is coloured against your own list, not against someone else's average.
